Pilau Recipe

Description

Have you ever wondered how fragrant, aromatic East African Swahili Pilau is made? Well I'm going to take you step by step on how to cook a flavorful pot

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice: 2 cups (rinsed and soaked for 20 minutes)
  • Meat (beef or chicken, cubed): 500g
  • Pilau masala spice: 2 tablespoons
  • Red onions (thinly sliced): 2 large
  • Garlic (minced): 1 tablespoon
  • Ginger (minced): 1 tablespoon
  • Cooking oil: 4 to 5 tablespoons
  • Tomato paste: 2 tablespoons (for rich color and depth)
  • Beef or chicken stock (or hot water): 3.5 to 4 cups
  • Salt: To taste

Steps

  1. Prep and Cook the Meat:

    Boil the cubed meat with a little garlic, ginger, and salt in a pot until tender and its water dries up completely. Set aside.

  2. Caramelize the Onions:

    The secret to rich dark color. In your large cooking pot, heat the cooking oil and add the sliced onions. Fry them steadily over medium heat until they turn deeply golden-brown and caramelized (be careful not to burn them).

  3. Add Aromatics and Spices:

    Stir in the remaining minced garlic, ginger, pilau masala, and tomato paste. Cook for about 2 minutes while stirring continuously until the spices become deeply aromatic.

  4. Combine Meat and Rice:

    Add the cooked meat into the spiced onion mixture and stir well. Pour in the drained basmati rice and gently toss so every grain is coated in the spiced oil.

  5. Pour Liquid and Boil:

    Measure in 3.5 to 4 cups of hot stock or water and add salt to taste. Bring it to a rolling boil over medium-high heat until most of the liquid is absorbed at the surface.

  6. Simmer and Steam:

    Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot tightly with a lid or foil, and let it steam undisturbed for 15 minutes. Fluff gently with a fork and serve hot with kachumbari.
